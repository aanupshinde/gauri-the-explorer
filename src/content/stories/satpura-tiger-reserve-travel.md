#### **Introduction:**

Today, let us explore **Satpura Tiger Reserve,** which is situated in the **Hoshangabad** (Narmadapuram) district of Madhya Pradesh, India. It is one of the most beautiful and diverse wildlife sanctuaries in the country. Spread over an area of 1,427 sq. km., the reserve is home to a plethora of flora and fauna, including tigers, leopards, sloth bears, wild dogs, and various species of birds.

#### **Flora & Fauna:**

The Satpura Tiger Reserve is covered with dense forests and grasslands and is home to a large number of species of plants and animals. The most notable species of animals found in the reserve are tigers, leopards, sloth bears, wild dogs, and Indian Bison. The reserve is also known for its birdlife, with over 300 species of birds found here. The vegetation here is mainly comprised of teak, sal, tendu, bamboo, and mahua trees.

#### **Zones in Satpura Tiger Reserve:**

The Satpura Tiger Reserve is divided into the following zones:

1.  Bison Zone
2.  Madhai Zone
3.  Pachmarhi Zone
4.  Bori-Satpur Zone
5.  Khandwa Zone

Zones are also classified as Madhai, Jamanidev, Parsapaani, Churna and Panchmarhi. Each zone offers a unique experience to visitors.

#### **Why should you visit Satpura Tiger Reserve?:**

The Satpura Tiger Reserve is a unique destination that offers a perfect blend of adventure, nature, and wildlife. Unlike other wildlife sanctuaries in India, the Satpura Tiger Reserve allows visitors to explore the forest on foot, which is a thrilling experience. Additionally, the park offers night safaris, which is an excellent opportunity to spot nocturnal animals

Satpura National Park, renowned for its unique terrain and waterways, offers a plethora of endangered and migrant birds during the winter season. Species such as:

#### **Ways to Reach:**

The nearest airport to the Satpura Tiger Reserve is **Raja Bhoj Airport** in Bhopal, which is around 170 km away.

The reserve is also well-connected by road and rail. The nearest railway station is in **Pipariya**, which is around 50 km. away from the reserve.

Visitors can hire a taxi or take a bus from Bhopal to reach the Satpura Tiger Reserve. Once in the reserve, visitors can explore the forest on foot or take a jeep safari

#### **Safari Services:**

The Satpura Tiger Reserve offers jeep safaris, elephant safaris, and boat safaris to visitors. The cost of a jeep safari ranges from INR 2,500 to INR 4,000 per vehicle, depending on the zone. The cost of an elephant safari is around INR 3,500 per person, and the cost of a boat safari is around INR 2,500 per person

#### **Is it Budget-Friendly?**

The total cost of the trip per person depends on the mode of transport, accommodation, and the number of safaris taken. On average, the cost of a 2N/3D day trip to the Satpura Tiger Reserve ranges from INR 7,000 to INR 10,000 per person.

#### **Conclusion:**

The Satpura Tiger Reserve is a must-visit destination for nature and wildlife enthusiasts. With its diverse flora and fauna, five unique zones, and thrilling safari experiences, it offers a once-in-a-lifetime experience that should not be missed
