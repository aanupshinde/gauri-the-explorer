About this blog

It's a blog about the diverse and unique culture of Bharat. The country's tourism is growing because of its natural flora and fauna, its scenic beauty and topography, its food, one of the oldest civilizations, ancient history, vivid religious practices and its many spoken languages. Bharat is growing and developing vigorously as an Incredible Nation!

What I write about

  • Spiritual journeys: Varanasi, Haridwar, Rishikesh and ancient temples
  • Wildlife: national parks, tiger reserves and bird sanctuaries
  • Heritage: forts, UNESCO sites and India's culture
  • Explore India: itineraries, getaways and travel tips
